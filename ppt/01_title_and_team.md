## Presenter Notes
- Welcome the audience and introduce NovaCPP as a next-generation web application framework built in standard C++17.
- Emphasize that while modern web stacks are heavily fragmented into multi-tier microservices and bloated JavaScript runtimes, NovaCPP unifies frontend rendering, reactive state, database persistence, and agentic AI loops into a single high-performance binary.
- Mention that this presentation covers the complete architectural lifecycle: from the core problems in modern web development to the mathematical guarantees of the Pregel AI graph engine, culminating in real-world benchmark verification and business feasibility.
